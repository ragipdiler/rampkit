# Rampkit logo

The mark shows three equally sized rounded swatches stepping upward: chosen colors becoming a coherent ramp. It uses Rampkit's existing neutral palette and pairs with the existing system-ui wordmark.

- Master SVG: `apps/web/public/brand/rampkit-mark.svg` (24 × 24 viewBox).
- Toolbar: 20 × 20px, 8px gap, system-ui 14px / 500 wordmark.
- Browser icon: `apps/web/app/icon.svg`, matching the master mark.
- Solid fills: #8E8E96, #626268, #242426. No gradients, shadows, outlines or animation.
- Keep the aspect ratio and leave at least 4px clear space. Use at least 16px for the mark.
- The mark is original Rampkit artwork under the repository's MIT license. The GitHub profile logo remains a separate third-party brand asset.

Keep the master mark and app icon synchronized when updating the logo. Other UI icons remain Lucide.
